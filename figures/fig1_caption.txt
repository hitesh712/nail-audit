Fig. 1. Representative images from each of the six classes, drawn from the test partition. For each class the images shown are those carrying the least rotation fill among correctly classified examples, so that nail morphology rather than augmentation artefact is visible; they are not a random sample. Characteristic features are nonetheless apparent: the longitudinal pigmented band of acral lentiginous melanoma, the bulbous distal phalanx of clubbing, and the punctate depressions of pitting. The figure also illustrates the synthetic augmentation present throughout the distributed collection - visible rotation, composited rectangular patches, and colour and noise perturbation - which motivates the occlusion control reported in Section 5.6. Images are from the Nail Disease Detection dataset [9], reproduced under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
